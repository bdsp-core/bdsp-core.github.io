    <h1>Work with us</h1>
    <p class="rd-lede rd-measure">We are open for postdoc and engineering applications related to our CAISR (&ldquo;Complete AI Sleep Report&rdquo;) grant. The positions are mostly about machine learning and signal processing problems in sleep medicine and neurology.</p>
    <p class="rd-measure">We are looking for new group members with passion, talent and grit. You will work on the grand challenges of medical AI and big data, at the interface of physiology and machine learning: deciding which questions matter, creating and improving methods, making discoveries, and bringing them into clinical use.</p>
